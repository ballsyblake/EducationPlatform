The season at a glance

Overview

Three roles touch an assessment, and they hand it between them in a fixed order. The Club Development Unit opens the season and closes it; clubs supply their own data; assessors score. Nobody skips a step, and only the Unit can move an assessment backwards.

1

Open the season

Club Development Unit

A cycle is one season’s assessments. It starts in SETUP, where clubs can’t see it, so the Unit can build the season out before anyone is invited in.

The Unit does

  • Creates the cycle for the year, from the board at /cda/cdu.
  • Adds each affiliated club with its zone and tier.
  • Creates one administrator account per club and hands over the sign‑in link shown on screen.
  • Creates assessor accounts, and groups clubs into pools.
  • Allocates every line item across each pool — two assessors per item, plus a third only to break a split.
  • Moves the cycle to CLUB_ENTRY when clubs should start.

The portal does

  • Loads Football Queensland’s rubric automatically — 54 line items, the nine Non‑Negotiables, the qualification ladder and the structure standards. Nothing to configure.
  • Gives the new cycle its per‑shield structure standards.
  • Opens an assessment for every club in the cycle, with a blank result row for each Non‑Negotiable.
  • Sets each shield’s bar: Gold 75%, Silver 55%, Bronze 40%.
Sign-in links are single use and shown once. Hand over the link the Clubs page displays. A new administrator asking for their own on the login page will be told one is on its way and get nothing, because the invite already issued is still live.
2

The club submits its data

Club administrator

Four sections, all editable until the club presses submit. After that the club can read everything and change nothing — only the Unit can reopen it.

SectionWhat it holdsWhat it feeds
Staff register Every technical role, with qualification, years, employment basis and Blue Card The whole Technical Qualifications domain
Club structure Eleven organisational functions, who holds them, and their diploma NN7 — computed, not judged
Non‑Negotiables A yes or not‑yet against each of the nine, with evidence attached Eligibility, and the shield cap
Participation Registration, retention, female and youth figures Outcomes line items
The structure page is the one place a club sees its own score as it works. Every other number stays hidden until release, because every other number is an assessor’s judgement. This one is a published rule, so a club that learns in March that Gold needs a qualified Head of Individual Development can appoint one before the season starts.
3

Assessors score, one line item at a time

Assessor

An assessor does not take a club. They take one line item across a whole pool of clubs — every club’s Playing Style, say, one after another. That is how Football Queensland allocates, and it is what makes two assessors comparable: they are judging the same thing repeatedly rather than different things once.

The assessor sees

  • Their allocated line items, and for each, the list of clubs in that pool.
  • The club’s evidence and submitted documents.
  • A 0–3 or 0–4 scale with Football Queensland’s evidence bands.

The assessor never sees

  • What the other assessor on the same item scored.
  • Any club’s total, percentage or shield.
  • Clubs outside the pools they hold an item in.
4

The Unit reconciles

Club Development Unit

Two independent scores per line item become one. The reconcile screen opens on the disagreements rather than on all 54 rows, because the job is getting through the splits and a full list of settled ones is how the two that matter get lost.

FlagMeaningWhat the Unit does
AgreedBoth assessors gave the same scoreAccept in bulk
1 star apartA minor splitResolve, usually to the median
2+ stars apartA major splitRead both comments, decide, and record why
Not scoredNobody was allocated the itemAllocate it — it counts as zero otherwise

The Unit also verifies each of the nine Non‑Negotiables, and writes a paragraph of feedback per macro‑area — the part of the report a club can actually act on. A domain percentage says Delivery was 63%; an area note says match day was the problem and training wasn’t.

Departing from a suggestion always costs a sentence. Overriding the assessors’ median needs a rationale, and so does departing from NN7’s computed level. Judgement is allowed; it just has to be visible as judgement rather than indistinguishable from arithmetic.
5

The rating is computed

The portal

Every line item contributes score × weight to one grand total. A domain’s influence is however many points it happens to hold — not a share anybody configured. Delivery is 37% of the rating because 490 of the 1,332 points sit there.

Tier 2 clubs are assessed on 18 of the same coded line items, with Technical scaled to 112 points, so their percentage comes from a denominator they were actually assessed against.

6

Release, review, confirm

All three roles

Locking freezes the result; releasing shows it to the club. What the club receives is a preliminary rating — Football Queensland is explicit that a club may only communicate its rating by displaying its Club Shield once that rating is confirmed.

Technical Qualifications cannot be reviewed. It is computed from the staff register rather than judged by an assessor, so a wrong Technical score is fixed by correcting the register — there is no opinion to appeal.

Three moves run backwards, all Unit‑only: reopen returns a submission to the club, unlock clears the frozen result and returns to reconciling, and withdraw takes a released rating back from the club. Withdrawal is refused while a review is open.
